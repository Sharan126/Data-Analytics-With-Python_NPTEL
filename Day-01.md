## Day-01: Introduction to data  

⚠️ Important Warning in Analytics (Most Students Mistake)
The danger in using quantitative methods is **not** calculation.
The real threat is lack of fundamental understanding of:

1. **Why** to use a particular technique/procedure  
2. **How** to use it correctly  
3. **How** to interpret the result correctly

## 📊 4 Levels of Data Measurement

| Level | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Nominal** | Categories only (no order) | Blood group, Gender |
| **Ordinal** | Ordered categories | Ratings (Good/Better/Best) |
| **Interval** | Fixed difference but no true zero | Temperature (°C) |
| **Ratio** | True zero exists | Height, Weight, Income |
